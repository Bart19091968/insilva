Bio

Plain-spoken. Calm under complexity.

27 years in ICT. 24 of those in IT infrastructure. 21 leading teams. Today Bart is Application Manager for the Product domain at Colruyt Group, running functional and technical support teams across Belgium and India.

Before that: Service Delivery Manager at Econocom (managing nearshore operations in Morocco and Romania), ICT Operations Manager at Katoen Natie, Corporate ICT Coordinator at CFE Group, and IT Technology & Services Manager for the same group from 2004. The career began as a network and system engineer in the late nineties.

The work has a consistent shape: align IT to the business, run things by ITIL where ITIL helps, manage incidents and crises calmly, negotiate sensibly with vendors, and leave operational governance that doesn't collapse when the consultant walks out the door.

In Silva is the practice he built around that experience. The name comes from the Latin in silva — "in the forest". It captures both the territory (digital complexity, several stakeholders, no obvious path) and the role (a guide who has walked it before — not a guru with a printed map).

Working principles

How we show up.

01.

Guide, not guru.

We walk alongside. We point out the path, name the trade-offs, and leave teams stronger than we found them.

02.

Curious before prescriptive.

No engagement starts with a template. We ask questions before recommending models, and we trust your context.

03.

Honest about trade-offs.

Frameworks aren't religion. We tell you what won't work, not just what will. SIAM is not for everyone.

04.

Calm under complexity.

Multi-provider transitions are messy. We don't add to the noise — we help you find the signal in it.

05.

Plain language.

If we can't explain it without buzzwords, we probably don't understand it yet. The lectures on this site are evidence.

06.

Knowledge that travels.

The point is for your team to keep doing the work after we're gone. Workshops, runbooks and clear documentation are part of every engagement.
