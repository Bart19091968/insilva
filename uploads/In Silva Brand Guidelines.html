Brand essence
02
in silva = in the forest

Guide, not guru

We walk alongside our clients. We point out the path; we don't lecture from a podium.

Plain language, calm tone, expert footing. Digital complexity made navigable.

Quiet confidence

Evergreen, not flashy. We're the steady tree on the trail, not the neon sign.

The logo
03
1

The mark — circle & tree

A pine inside a clearing. The arrow-trunk reads as growth and direction; the circle as a whole, considered view.

2

Wordmark — lowercase, humanist

Set in Lato Black. Lowercase makes us approachable; the geometric construction keeps it serious.

3

The underline

A grounded baseline beneath "consulting" — a path on the forest floor. Optional in compact lockups.

4

Tagline lockup

"helping you through the digital forest" sits below the underline at one-third the wordmark height.

Clear space & variants
04

Give the mark room to breathe.

Clear space
= height of the mark ÷ 2

Minimum clear space

Keep an exclusion zone equal to half the mark's height on every side. No type or imagery enters this zone.

Minimum size

Digital: 120 px wide for the full lockup; 32 px for the mark alone. Print: 30 mm wide minimum.

Background contrast

Place on paper, white, deep forest, or charcoal. Avoid placing on busy photography without a scrim.

Logo on backgrounds
05

Four approved surfaces.

Voice & tone
09

How we sound.

Calm, plain, expert. We name the trees by their proper names but we don't show off. A trusted hiking partner — not a tour bus megaphone.

We are

  • Plainspoken & concrete
  • Calm under complexity
  • Honest about trade-offs
  • Curious before prescriptive
  • Warm, never folksy

We are not

  • Buzzword-heavy
  • "Disruptive" or "10×"
  • Salesy or pushy
  • Vague or hedging
  • Over-formal corporate
Graphic language
10

Three supporting devices.

The circle motif

Use the open circle from the mark as a framing device — for portraits, callouts, page numbers, and section openers.

Tree as bullet

Replace bullets with a small tree silhouette for branded lists.

The grounded line

The underline beneath "consulting" extends as a structural rule across layouts.

Generous white

Warm paper, deep margins, never crowded.

Dotted clearing

A soft dot grid as background texture for openers and dividers.
