What we do.

Five practices, applied separately or together depending on where the organization is on its journey. Each engagement is shaped around your context — there is no off-the-shelf SIAM.

01

IT interim management.

Day-to-day management of your IT organization when continuity matters — through transformation, crisis, or while you hire. We step in, take ownership, and leave the organization steadier than we found it. Result-oriented, practical, a "can do" approach.

  • Day-to-day IT management
  • IT transformation management
  • IT crisis management
  • IT strategy & business alignment
  • IT governance design
  • M&A — due diligence to integration
  • Vendor contract negotiation
  • IT infrastructure optimization
02

SIAM implementation.

Service integration across multi-sourced ecosystems. We help you decide whether SIAM fits, choose the right structure (internal, external, lead supplier, hybrid), and stand up the governance and processes that make it real. Most engagements start with a Discovery & Strategy phase before any contract is touched.

  • Maturity assessment & outline business case
  • SIAM model design & structure choice
  • Boards, forums & working group setup
  • Provider onboarding & transition
  • End-to-end measurement & reporting framework
03

ITSM process design.

ITIL-aligned processes that fit your organization's size and culture — not a textbook. Incident, problem, change and request management designed to be practiced, not just documented. We focus on what people actually do on a Tuesday afternoon.

  • Incident & problem management
  • Change & release management
  • Service request fulfillment
  • Service catalog & portfolio
  • CMDB & configuration management
04

Service delivery management.

Day-to-day operational governance for application and infrastructure services. SLAs that mean something, reports people actually read, and the difficult conversations with providers when something isn't working. Often an interim or fractional role.

  • Service performance reviews
  • SLA design & SLA reporting
  • Major incident management
  • Continual improvement programs
  • Stakeholder & vendor relationship management
05

Training & workshops.

Foundation-level lectures on ITSM and SIAM, plus bespoke workshops that build shared vocabulary across business and IT. Calm, plain-language, conversational — the same tone as the readers on this site. We deliver these on-site, remote, or as keynote sessions.

  • SIAM Foundation (~90 min)
  • ITSM Essentials (~60 min)
  • Tailored half-day team workshops
  • Maturity assessment workshops
  • Keynote & conference sessions
